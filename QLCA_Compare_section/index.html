Factor Overview

The Building type factor describes the configuration of residential units and the overall building volume.

The Density factor describes the number of buildings and storeys within a defined settlement unit. Together with the Building Type factor, it defines the spatial structure of the urban layout.

The Road layout factor includes three configurations: grid, curved roads, and cul-de-sacs, representing common street network patterns across different urban forms.

The Construction method factor distinguishes between timber and mineral construction, representing two common structural systems.

The Heat supply factor includes natural gas, local energy systems, and heat pumps. It covers the provision of thermal energy for space heating and domestic hot water, including generation, distribution, and transfer.

The Energy efficiency level factor describes the annual heating demand per square metre of usable floor area (kWh/m² usable floor area *a).
