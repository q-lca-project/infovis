# The Neighbourhood as a System: Exploring how urban design choices shape life cycle emissions

This information visualization project explores the Quarter Life Cycle Assessment (Q-LCA) model, which examines how urban design decisions influence the climate impacts of residential neighbourhoods. It was developed during the summer semester of 2026 as part of the seminar “Visualizing Sustainability Models”, supervised by Dr. Francesca Morini and Erik Weiss at the University of Applied Sciences Potsdam. The interactive scrollytelling experience introduces the fundamentals and workings of the Q-LCA model and illustrates its application through a specific case.

A project by Claudia Keuß and Michelle Otiji.

[View project](https://q-lca-project.github.io/infovis/)
